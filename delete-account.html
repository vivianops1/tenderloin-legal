Delete Your Account & Data

This page explains how to delete your Tenderloin account and the data associated with it. It applies to the Tenderloin mobile app published by VivianOps LLC.

Most of Tenderloin works anonymously — you can create and join sessions without an account. Anonymous session data (your location for that session and your swipe votes) is temporary and is automatically deleted about 2 hours after the session ends. You only have a stored account if you chose to sign in with Google or Apple.

Option A — In the app

  1. Open Tenderloin and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Sign Out. This ends your session on the device and stops the app from associating new activity with your account.
  3. To fully remove the account and any stored history, email us as described in Option B below — signing out alone does not erase data already stored.

If you only ever used Tenderloin anonymously, there is nothing further to delete — your session data expires on its own.

Option B — Request full deletion by email

Email contact@vivianops.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your Google or Apple sign-in, and we will permanently delete your account and personal data.

What gets deleted

DeletedRetained
  • Your profile (name, email, profile photo from Google/Apple sign-in)
  • Your match history
  • Your saved dietary preferences
  • Anonymized, aggregated swipe data that contains no personal information and cannot be linked back to you
  • Purchase and subscription records we are legally required to keep for tax and accounting purposes

Processing time

We process deletion requests and remove your personal data within 30 days of receiving your request. If you have an active Gold subscription, manage or cancel it through your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings — deleting your Tenderloin account does not automatically cancel store-managed billing.
