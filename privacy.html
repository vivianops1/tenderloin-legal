Tenderloin — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 4, 2026

Tenderloin ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Tenderloin mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information (Optional)
If you choose to create an account using Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo as provided by those services. You may use the App anonymously without creating an account.

Location Data
When you create or join a session, the App accesses your device's location to find nearby restaurants. Location data is used only during your active session and is not stored after the session ends. We do not track your location in the background.

Session & Swipe Data
We collect your swipe choices (right/left) during active sessions to determine group matches. For logged-in users, we store match history (restaurant name and date) so you can view past results.

Anonymized Analytics
We do not currently run product analytics or third-party analytics tracking in the App. In the future, we may collect anonymized, aggregated swipe data — such as restaurant popularity by area, food type preferences, and group size trends — to understand how the App is used and improve the experience. Any such data would be aggregated and could not be linked back to individual users. We will update this policy before enabling analytics.

Device Information
We collect standard device information including device type, operating system version, and app version for crash reporting and compatibility purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate the core App functionality (finding restaurants, managing sessions, determining matches)
  • Display relevant restaurants based on your location and food type preferences
  • Show sponsored restaurant cards within your swipe deck (clearly labeled as "Sponsored")
  • Maintain match history for logged-in users
  • Improve the App through anonymized analytics
  • Process Gold subscription purchases through RevenueCat

3. How We Share Your Information

With Other Session Participants
Your display name and swipe results (match/no match) are shared with others in your session. Individual swipe choices on specific restaurants are not shared with other participants.

Sponsored Restaurants
Restaurants that purchase sponsored placement receive anonymized impression and engagement counts. They do not receive any information about individual users.

Anonymized Data Licensing
We may license anonymized, aggregated swipe trend data to third parties. This data contains no personal information and cannot identify individual users. It includes only aggregate patterns such as cuisine popularity by region.

Service Providers
We use the following third-party services to operate the App:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and real-time features
  • Google Places API — restaurant data and photos
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • PostHog — product analytics (not currently active; reserved for potential future anonymized analytics)
  • Railway — backend hosting

Referral Partners
When you tap a DoorDash or OpenTable button after a match, you are redirected to their platform. We may receive a referral fee. No personal data is shared with these partners — you interact with them directly.

Legal Requirements
We may disclose information if required by law or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

4. Data Retention

  • Anonymous session data is automatically deleted 2 hours after session expiration.
  • Match history for logged-in users is retained until you delete your account.
  • Anonymized analytics are retained indefinitely as they contain no personal information.
  • Account information is retained until you delete your account.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Use anonymously — You are never required to create an account.
  • Delete your account — Contact us at contact@vivianops.com to request account deletion. All personal data will be removed within 30 days. See our account deletion page for details.
  • Location permissions — You can revoke location access in your device settings at any time. The App requires location access to function.
  • Analytics — The App does not currently perform analytics tracking. If we enable anonymized analytics in the future, you may contact us to opt out.

6. Children's Privacy

Tenderloin is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), Row Level Security on our database, and secure authentication via Google and Apple OAuth. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or via email (if you have an account). Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
